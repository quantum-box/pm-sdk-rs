# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project space for everyone.
Contributors are expected to communicate with care, assume good intent, and keep
discussion focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when behavior does not meet this code. To report a concern, please
contact the project maintainers through the repository owner.
